Terms of Use

Overview

DueSnark is a subscription reminder app developed by Yihang Yin. This policy explains how the app handles information on your device.

DueSnark does not operate a developer-controlled server, does not collect personal information through the app, and does not use any developer-controlled backend or third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, payment details, or any other personally identifying information through the app. DueSnark has no account system and no login.

Local Device Data

DueSnark stores a small number of app preferences locally on your device using iOS's standard storage system. These include:

  • Your selected reminder theme
  • Whether you have enabled daily reminders
  • Internal app state flags used during updates

These local preferences are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any developer-controlled service. You can remove them entirely by deleting the app.

Purchases

DueSnark offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (the "DueSnark Launch Collection") that unlocks additional reminder themes. All purchases are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store your payment information. The app uses verified transaction information provided by Apple's StoreKit framework to determine whether premium features are unlocked on your device. This determination happens locally using Apple's system, without involving a developer server.

Apple's handling of your payment and account information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you grant permission, DueSnark schedules daily local reminder notifications. These notifications:

  • Are created entirely from content included in the app
  • Are scheduled and delivered by your device's operating system
  • Do not involve any developer server or network connection
  • Contain no personal information

You can disable notifications at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Links to Apple Services

DueSnark includes a button that opens Apple's subscription management service in the App Store app. DueSnark does not append or transmit any developer-collected personal information when opening this link, as no such developer backend exists. Interactions with Apple's services once you leave DueSnark are governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

DueSnark does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services. No third-party code is embedded in the app beyond Apple's own system frameworks.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data collected through the app. DueSnark does not collect personal data through the app to share.

Data Retention

Local app preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them. DueSnark does not send these local preferences to any developer-controlled server.

Children's Privacy

DueSnark does not collect personal information through the app, including from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in a new Effective Date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
